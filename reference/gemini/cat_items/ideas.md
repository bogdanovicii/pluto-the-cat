# Five cat items for Pluto (brainstorm, 2026-09-15)

Checked against what exists: Nine Lives, Puffed Up, Squeaky Toy, Coco Blue, Wet Food Can, Kibble Bowl, the three
guns, and the Laser Pointer / Box Fort synergies. None of these repeat a mechanic already in the mod.

| # | Item | Type | Subtitle | Effect | Pluto hook | Synergy idea |
|---|------|------|----------|--------|------------|--------------|
| 1 | Ball of Yarn | Active (throw, room recharge) | "Batted Under The Couch" | Thrown ball bounces off walls for 6 s, trailing a strand; enemies it touches are tangled (rooted 1.5 s, then slowed). Walk into it to bat it again. | Every cat vs. yarn | + Coco Blue: Coco chases and re-bats the ball |
| 2 | Catnip Pouch | Active (damage recharge) | "Zoomies" | 7 s of +40 % move speed and +25 % fire rate, dodge rolls leave a charm-afterimage; then 2 s "catnap" at -20 % speed. | Pluto's 3 am sprints | + Puffed Up: zoomies trigger puff at no cost |
| 3 | Jingle Bell Collar | Passive | "Belled" | Each dodge roll jingles: a small ring (2.5 tiles) erases enemy bullets, 4 s cooldown; the bell icon flashes when ready. Enemies are never surprised by Pluto (+5 % damage taken shown as flavour only). | Owners Bogdan and Bianca belling the hunter | + Squeaky Toy: jingle also squeaks, luring enemies |
| 4 | Hairball | Active (timed, 350 dmg recharge) | "Hack Hack Hack" | Pluto coughs a sticky hairball grenade; it bursts into a fur cloud that slows enemy bullets inside by 60 % for 4 s. | Grooming a long raccoon tail has a cost | + Wet Food Can: the cloud also charms |
| 5 | Scratching Post | Active (place, floor-wide one use per room) | "Sharpen Up" | Places a post; standing next to it scratches: +30 % damage and piercing shots while within 2 tiles, post lasts the room. | Claws before a fight | + Katana: slashes reflect bullets near the post |

Numbers are first-pass balance targets for PlutoConfig, not tuned.
